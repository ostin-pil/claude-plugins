## Context

Opened from a Cashtank session (its session 56) at the user's ask. The user found the Cashtank apprentice's new arm swing unnatural, and after a research pass asked to "review and study" four pages on game-animation principles (Wikipedia's twelve principles, Animost's 3D character movement, and two Game Developer articles) and to "cook skills/experts/judges to help us with the animation. Would be nice to share the knowledge with other projects". Picked in the picker: a new plugin in this marketplace, with a knowledge skill, an expert agent, a judge agent and a motion-path probe.

## What happened

1. **The `motion-craft` skill:** a lean SKILL.md setting the order of work (brief, pose sheet, key, measure, judge blind, show) and seven references: the twelve principles as games bend them (from the four pages, sourced by page), game motion (responsiveness, control frames, blending and layers, cycles, readability, determinism), walk and arm-swing numbers (Murray 1967, Yegian 2019, the Froude check, animators' timing), stepped and puppet motion, the pose-sheet method and template, the judge's rubric, and tools with their licence terms as of November 2026.
2. **The agents:** `animator` plans or reworks a motion as a pose sheet from a brief and the rig's code; `motion-judge` reviews recorded motion blind, from the brief and the evidence only, scoring each rubric check with evidence.
3. **The probe:** `motion_path.py` (distinct poses, cycle, reversals, bulge, loop openness, corners, spacing, lag, `--relative-to`), `motion_sheet.py` (contact sheet and onion skin, from a reel or a folder of frames), and `godot/bone_path_recorder.gd`. Pure Python plus Pillow, NumPy for the onion.
4. **Wiring:** the marketplace and README list it, the prose gate scans its README, CI gains a `motion-kit` job, and the local gate's manifest runs its tests.
5. **First use, on the Cashtank apprentice's walk:** the recorder and the probe measured the hand trailing its forearm by 0 frames, a nearly retraced path and 6 drawn poses per arm cycle. The blind judge and the animator, both run from these definitions by general-purpose agents (the plugin isn't installed yet), independently found the walk's real fault: turned 50° from the camera its legs strode at 1.44 m/s, a trot. What the first use taught went back into the kit: `--relative-to` (the body's bob put corners into every path), the onion skin's need for a still camera, and the stride multiplier of a turned figure.
